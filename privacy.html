Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous User ID — a random ID generated on your device to track your mining progress. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Wallet Address — only if you choose to connect a wallet (e.g. MetaMask). We never ask for, store, or have access to your seed phrase or private keys.
  • Mining Activity — your mined balance and referral count, stored securely via Supabase so your progress isn't lost.
  • Basic Ad Data — our advertising partners (see Section 3) may use cookies or device identifiers to show relevant ads.

2. How We Use This Information

Solely to operate the mining app: tracking your balance, applying referral bonuses, and displaying ads that fund the platform. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-Party Advertising

We use advertising networks (such as Adsterra and/or PropellerAds) to display ads, including rewarded ads that boost your mining speed. These networks may independently collect technical data (device type, approximate location, browsing behavior) per their own privacy policies to serve and measure ads. We do not control and are not responsible for their data practices.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your mining data is stored using Supabase, a secured cloud database. While we take reasonable measures to protect it, no online system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the project develops. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated terms.

7. Nature of the Token (Important)

PNU is currently in a Testnet Mining Phase. Balances shown are simulated/off-chain points tracked on our server — they are not yet a live blockchain asset. They are intended to convert to a real token after a future Mainnet Token Generation Event (TGE), which is not guaranteed to happen on any specific timeline.

8. Total Supply & Allocation

Total supply is fixed at 100,00,00,000 PNU: 50% reserved for user mining (transparently tracked and shown live in-app), and 50% reserved for the team/ecosystem under a vesting schedule. This allocation is public and will not change without notice.

9. No Financial Guarantee

Mining PNU does not guarantee any future monetary value. Cryptocurrency projects carry risk, including the possibility that a token never launches or has no market value. Do not treat mined balances as a financial investment.

10. Acceptable Use

Do not use bots, scripts, or multiple accounts to abuse the mining or referral system. We reserve the right to reset balances found to be obtained fraudulently.

11. Contact

For questions about these terms, reach out via the contact details listed on our app or social pages.
